How to Read This Calendar
The five moving parts of the campaign, colour-coded throughout this document

This calendar sequences four campaign tactics from the approved strategy — (A) the Cup of Joe music-led main video, (B) the community-model KOL partnership, (C) support-measure beneficiary stories, and (D) campus activation events — plus the paid media flights that carry them. Nothing is plotted at random: every drop is anchored either to an EU thematic date (ErasmusDays, Human Rights Day, Education Day, Women's Day) or a Philippine cultural moment (Christmas peak, Sinulog, EDSA, Women's Month) — and deliberately pulled away from the late-October election period and Undas.

⚠️
Two hard constraints shape the whole shape of this plan:
1. Election window. Barangay & SK Elections are scheduled for 2 Nov 2026 (also All Souls'/Undas). The official campaign period and result-day run through late October. Per the 2025 lesson-learned on operating in a "highly charged socio-political environment," the EU goes organic-only / non-political from ~26 Oct to ~4 Nov — no boosted political-adjacent creative, no activations. The democracy/governance theme is held until after the election (late Feb, around EDSA).
2. No TikTok on EU-owned ads. All paid media runs on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube only. KOLs and Cup of Joe still post organically to their own TikTok — that reach is free and on-brand, it is simply never behind EU ad spend.

05
Support Measures — The Proof Layer
Field-shot beneficiary stories from real EU projects of the last 5–7 years · what to capture, when to shoot, when to air, how to position

Positioning logic — why support measures come second

The music video and KOLs create awareness and good feeling; support measures supply the evidence that earns trust. So they are sequenced behind the launch wave: the audience first falls for the song, then — when they ask "but what has the EU actually done for us?" — the beneficiary stories are the receipts. They are EU-owned-channel organic content, lightly boosted (cheap engagement/traffic objective), and they are the campaign's primary driver of the 90K click-throughs KPI — every story links to a Global Gateway info page.

🎬
Production reality: these must be shot in the field before they air. Field shoots run Sept–Dec 2026 (front-loaded while crews mobilise); stories air Nov 2026 – Mar 2027. Shoot across Luzon · Visayas · Mindanao for national spread and to feed regional KOLs (e.g. Cebu story ↔ Eya Borja; Mindanao story ↔ Maymay).

The 7 capture categories

Air: Nov · pairs w/ Edit-Down #2

A · Green Transition & Climate

Renewable-energy sites, e-mobility, climate-resilient farming/fishing, biodiversity. Capture: a young farmer, fisherfolk or engineer whose livelihood improved through an EU green project. 30–60s vertical testimonial + B-roll of the installation.

Air: Jan · pairs w/ Edit-Down #3

B · Education · Erasmus+ · TVET

Erasmus+ alumni, scholars, skills-training graduates. Capture: "from [province] to Europe and back" — a scholar's arc and what they're building at home now. The single strongest proof story; lead it on Int'l Day of Education.

Air: Feb · pairs w/ Edit-Down #4

C · Digital & Connectivity · Jobs

Digital-skills grads, start-ups, the Global Gateway digital package. Capture: a young digital entrepreneur or coder whose job exists because of EU-backed connectivity. Tie to the "digital connectivity hub" narrative.

Air: Feb–Mar · pairs w/ FTA + Women's Month

D · Trade · Livelihood · MSMEs

EU-supported MSMEs and value chains — coffee & cacao especially (a neat wink to a band named Cup of Joe). Capture: a woman-led co-op or exporter benefiting from EU trade support.

Air: late Feb · pairs w/ Edit-Down #5 (post-election)

E · Governance · Rights · Peace

Rule-of-law, civil-society, BARMM/Mindanao peace-building. Most sensitive — frame around people, not politics; hold until after the election and air around EDSA / Human Rights week with careful, neutral language.

Air: always-on B-roll

F · Youth · Culture · People-to-People

Youth exchange, cultural & sports cooperation. Cross-cutting connective tissue between heavier stories; great for Reels/Stories filler and activation recaps.

Air: Mar · Women's Month / IWD

G · Gender · Health · Inclusion

Gender-equality programmes, health, disability inclusion. Capture: a woman or PWD whose opportunity opened through an EU programme — anchors the Women's-Month finale and the recap cut.

Format spec for all: 30–60s vertical, real Filipino voice in conversational Filipino, location super, EU + Global Gateway lockup, closing line "Ito ang EU — para sa atin," and a link/CTA to the Global Gateway info page.
